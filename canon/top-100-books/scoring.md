# Top 100 Scoring

Per-criterion scores for every work in
[the Top 100](README.md), version 1.0. Each criterion is scored 1 to
10; the total is the weighted sum on a 100-point scale.

Columns: **Inf** civilizational influence (25%), **Ach** literary and
intellectual achievement (20%), **End** endurance (15%), **Rch**
global reach (15%), **Trd** importance within its own tradition
(15%), **Sch** scholarly consensus (10%).

Scores are the Canon Editor agent's judgement from the general
scholarly record, not from a new evidence dossier per work. They are
proposals for review, and the G3 and G4 reviews may move them.
