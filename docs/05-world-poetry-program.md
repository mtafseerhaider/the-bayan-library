# World Poetry Program

## Purpose

The World Poetry Program exists to preserve, organize, study, and connect the
greatest poetic traditions, poets, poems, and literary movements in human
history.

Poetry is treated as one of the highest forms of human expression and a
foundational pillar of The Bayan Library.

The program promotes both appreciation and scholarly understanding of poetry
across civilizations, languages, and historical periods.

## Mission

The mission of the World Poetry Program is to:

- Preserve major poetic works.
- Document literary traditions.
- Connect poets across cultures.
- Create accessible reading pathways.
- Build a global poetry canon.
- Support comparative literary study.

The program should help readers discover how poetry has shaped human
civilization.

## Scope

The program includes:

- Epic poetry
- Lyric poetry
- Mystical poetry
- Religious poetry
- Philosophical poetry
- Narrative poetry
- Love poetry
- Political poetry
- Modern poetry
- Contemporary poetry

Both individual poems and complete collections are within scope.

## Core Principles

The World Poetry Program follows these principles:

- Literary excellence
- Historical awareness
- Civilizational respect
- Global representation
- Comparative scholarship
- Long-term relevance

Poetry should be studied both within its own tradition and within the broader
history of world literature.

## Translation Considerations

Many readers encounter poetry through translation.

When evaluating translated works:

- Recognize translation limitations.
- Document major translators.
- Compare multiple translations when possible.
- Distinguish between original language significance and translation quality.

Translation history is part of a work's influence.

## Comparative Poetry Studies

The repository should encourage meaningful comparisons between traditions.

Examples include:

- Rumi and Dante
- Hafez and Shakespeare
- Ghalib and Goethe
- Iqbal and Nietzsche
- Basho and Wordsworth

Comparisons should illuminate similarities and differences without forcing
equivalence.

## Success Criteria

The World Poetry Program succeeds when it:

- Preserves major poetic traditions.
- Supports serious study.
- Promotes global literary understanding.
- Encourages exploration across cultures.
- Documents influence and continuity.
- Maintains scholarly credibility.

Its purpose is not simply to collect poems but to preserve humanity's poetic
heritage.

## Program Summary

The World Poetry Program serves as a comprehensive map of humanity's poetic
imagination.

It connects languages, cultures, civilizations, and generations through the
study of poetry and ensures that the world's greatest poetic achievements remain
accessible, organized, and meaningful for future readers.
