# Islamic Civilization Program

## Purpose

The Islamic Civilization Program exists to preserve, organize, study, and
connect the intellectual, spiritual, literary, philosophical, scientific, legal,
and historical contributions of Islamic civilization.

It serves as one of the primary pillars of The Bayan Library.

The program is intended to be scholarly, historically grounded, intellectually
rigorous, and non-sectarian.

Its purpose is understanding rather than advocacy.

## Mission

The mission of the Islamic Civilization Program is to:

- Preserve important works.
- Document historical developments.
- Connect intellectual traditions.
- Create accessible learning pathways.
- Build an interconnected knowledge graph.
- Support comparative scholarship.

The program should help readers understand how Islamic civilization contributed
to the development of human knowledge.

## Scope

The program covers:

- Theology
- Philosophy

- Jurisprudence
- History
- Literature
- Poetry

## Core Principles

The program follows several guiding principles:

- Intellectual honesty.
- Historical accuracy.
- Civilizational respect.

- Comparative understanding.
- Methodological transparency.

All major perspectives should be represented fairly.

- Al-Farabi

## Success Criteria

The Islamic Civilization Program succeeds when it:

- Preserves major intellectual achievements.
- Improves accessibility.
- Encourages serious study.
- Connects ideas across centuries.
- Supports comparative scholarship.
- Maintains historical accuracy.

The objective is to build one of the most organized and intellectually useful
digital collections on Islamic civilization.

## Program Summary

The Islamic Civilization Program serves as a comprehensive map of Islamic
intellectual history.

Its role is to preserve knowledge, support learning, document influence, and
connect readers with one of the most significant intellectual traditions in
human civilization.
