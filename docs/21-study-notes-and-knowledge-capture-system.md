# Study Notes and Knowledge Capture System

## Purpose

This document defines how knowledge should be captured, explained, simplified,
retained, connected, and applied within The Bayan Library.

The objective is not merely to collect notes.

The objective is to transform reading into understanding and understanding into
wisdom.

Every note should make future learning easier.

## Core Principle

For every significant idea ask:

- What does it mean?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it connect to other ideas?
- How can it be applied in real life?
- How can it be explained to a child?

Understanding is not complete until you can explain it simply.

## Knowledge Capture Workflow

For every book, article, lecture, course, paper, or discussion:

```text

↓

↓

↓
Important Vocabulary
↓

↓

↓
```

Every stage adds value.

## The Two-Layer Explanation System

Every important concept should be explained at two levels.

## Level 1: Explain for an 8-Year-Old

Purpose:

Ensure true understanding.

Requirements:

- Short sentences
- Simple vocabulary
- Familiar examples
- No technical jargon

Template:

```markdown
## Explain Like I'm 8

Imagine...

Think about...

This is like...
```

Example:

### Epistemology

Explain Like I'm 8:

"Epistemology is the study of how we know things.

For example, if someone tells you a tiger is outside your house, how do you know
if it is true?

You might look outside, ask someone else, or check a camera.

Epistemology is about figuring out how people learn what is true."

## Level 2: General Explanation

Purpose:

Provide an explanation accessible to most readers.

Requirements:

- Plain language
- Minimal jargon
- Real-world examples

Template:

```markdown
## General Explanation

Definition

Why It Matters

Example

Related Concepts
```

## Technical Terms and Footnotes

Whenever technical terms are necessary:

Use footnotes.

Example:

```markdown
Epistemology[^1] is the study of knowledge.

[^1]: Epistemology is the branch of philosophy that investigates how knowledge
    is acquired, justified, and evaluated.
```

Readers should never need a philosophy degree to understand notes.

## Vocabulary Capture System

Every major concept should maintain a vocabulary record.

Template:

```markdown

## Definition

## Simple Meaning

## Origin

## Related Words

## Examples

## Books That Use This Term
```

Example:

### Ontology

Simple Meaning:

"What kinds of things exist?"

General Meaning:

"Ontology studies the nature of existence and reality."

Example:

"Do numbers exist?

Do ideas exist?

Does time exist?

Ontology asks questions like these."

## Real-Life Application Framework

For every concept ask:

- Where does this appear in real life?
- How can I use it?
- How can it improve decisions?
- How can it improve character?
- How can it solve problems?

Learning should lead to action.

## Reflection Questions

After studying ask:

- What surprised me?
- What changed my mind?
- What do I disagree with?
- What deserves further study?
- What should I apply?

Reflection converts information into understanding.

## Wisdom Extraction Framework

For every note identify:

## Success Criteria

The system succeeds when:

- Complex ideas become understandable.
- Knowledge becomes memorable.
- Learning becomes actionable.
- Notes remain useful years later.
- Future learning becomes easier.

## System Summary

The Study Notes and Knowledge Capture System transforms books, articles,
lectures, and experiences into a permanent network of understanding.

The highest goal is not collecting notes.

The highest goal is making wisdom easier to discover, remember, and apply
throughout life.
