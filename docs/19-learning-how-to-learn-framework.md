# Learning How to Learn Framework

## Purpose

The Learning How to Learn Framework teaches readers how to extract
understanding, retain knowledge, connect ideas, and apply wisdom from books and
other sources.

It teaches readers how to absorb, retain, connect, evaluate, and apply knowledge
from books, articles, lectures, research papers, and other intellectual works.

The purpose is not merely to read more books.

The purpose is to learn more effectively from every book.

The goal is not simply to read more.

The goal is to understand more, remember more, and apply more.

## Mission

The framework transforms:

```text
Information
↓
Knowledge
↓
Understanding
↓
Wisdom
↓
Action
↓
Character
```

Learning should ultimately change behaviour and judgment.

## Core Principle

Before studying or reading any work, understand:

- Its language
- Its context
- Its author
- Its assumptions
- Its audience
- Its methodology
- Its vocabulary
- Its objectives

Preparation improves comprehension.

## Foundational Intellectual Concepts

Readers should gradually become familiar with:

- Epistemology
- Ontology
- Metaphysics
- Logic
- Ethics
- Hermeneutics
- Phenomenology
- Political Philosophy

These concepts act as intellectual tools for interpreting knowledge.

## Author Decoding Framework

Before reading identify:

- What problem is the author trying to solve?
- Who was the intended audience?
- What assumptions does the author make?
- What historical events influenced the author?
- What intellectual tradition is being represented?

Understanding the author often matters as much as understanding the text.

## Reading Modes

### Survey Reading

Purpose:

Understand overall structure quickly.

Focus:

- Table of contents
- Introduction
- Conclusion
- Chapter summaries

### Analytical Reading

Purpose:

Understand arguments deeply.

Focus:

- Claims
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Counterarguments

### Syntopical Reading

Purpose:

Compare multiple works.

Examples:

- Plato and Aristotle
- Ibn Sina and Mulla Sadra
- Ghalib and Iqbal

### Research Reading

Purpose:

Create and generate new understanding.

Focus:

- Synthesis
- Evaluation
- Knowledge creation

## Knowledge Extraction Framework

For every book or work identify:

- Main (central) idea
- Most important insight
- Key arguments
- Important questions raised
- Useful practical applications
- Related ideas

## Memory Framework

Methods:

- Active recall
- Spaced repetition
- Note-taking
- Teaching others
- Writing summaries (summarization)
- Cross-linking
- Knowledge graph linking

Memory improves through retrieval.

## Application Framework

Ask:

- What is true?
- What is useful?
- What can I apply today?
- What should I do differently?
- What changes should I make?
- What action follows from this?

Knowledge should lead to action.

## Wisdom Extraction Framework

For every major work ask:

- What changed my mind?
- What challenged me?
- What challenged my assumptions?
- What improved my understanding?
- What should I remember in ten years?
- What insight deserves lifelong remembrance?
- What should I apply immediately?

## Personal Knowledge System

Every completed work should generate:

- Summary
- Notes
- Questions
- Applications
- Related ideas

Learning should accumulate.
