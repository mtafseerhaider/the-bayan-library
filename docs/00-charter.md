# The Bayan Library

## Foundational Charter

> "He taught him clear expression." (Qur'an 55:4)

Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

The Bayan Library is a hybrid personal and public knowledge repository dedicated
to preserving, organizing, studying, and connecting humanity's greatest
intellectual, literary, philosophical, scientific, and spiritual achievements.

Its purpose is to serve simultaneously as:

- A personal lifelong learning system
- A digital commonplace book
- A research archive
- A world literature collection
- A map of civilization
- A knowledge graph
- A public intellectual resource

The repository is maintained primarily for the intellectual development of its
owner, while remaining open to public benefit and contribution.

---

## Vision

The long-term vision of The Bayan Library is to become a modern digital
equivalent of:

- The Library of Alexandria
- Bayt al-Hikma (House of Wisdom)
- The Great Books Movement
- A Zettelkasten
- A scholarly digital humanities project

The goal is not merely to collect information.

The goal is to preserve wisdom.

---

## Core Belief

Human civilization is a continuous conversation.

- Truth

The Bayan Library exists to document and explore that conversation.

## Foundational Principles

### 1. Intellectual Honesty

Arguments must be represented fairly and accurately.

No thinker shall be misrepresented.

---

### 2. Civilizational Respect

Every civilization shall be treated as a source of potential wisdom.

No civilization serves as the default standard by which all others are judged.

---

### 3. Scholarly Rigor

Claims should be supported by reputable scholarship whenever possible.

Popular opinion alone is insufficient.

---

### 4. Long-Term Thinking

The repository is intended to remain useful for decades.

Preference should be given to durability over novelty.

---

### 5. Comparability

Ideas from different traditions should be compared respectfully and
systematically.

---

### 6. Accessibility

Complex ideas should be explained clearly.

The pursuit of scholarship should never require unnecessary obscurity.

---

### 7. Continuous Improvement

The repository is never complete.

Each addition should improve:

- Accuracy
- Coverage
- Organization
- Insight
- Accessibility

---

## Hybrid Governance Model

The Bayan Library follows a hybrid governance model.

### Personal Repository First

The library exists primarily for the repository owner.

Editorial direction remains under the owner's control.

---

### Public Repository Second

External contributions are welcome.

Constructive discussion is encouraged.

Community knowledge is valued.

---

### Editorial Authority

Final editorial authority belongs to the repository owner.

Contributions may be:

- Accepted
- Modified
- Reorganized
- Rejected

based on repository goals and standards.
