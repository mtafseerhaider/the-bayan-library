# Quran Lifelong Learning Program

## Purpose

The Quran Lifelong Learning Program establishes a structured, multi-decade
framework for studying, understanding, reflecting upon, and applying the Quran
throughout life.

The objective is to treat the Quran not merely as a text to be read, but as a
lifelong intellectual, spiritual, civilizational, linguistic, ethical, and
practical companion.

The Quran is approached simultaneously as:

- Revelation
- Linguistic masterpiece
- Civilizational text
- Philosophical source
- Spiritual guide
- Ethical framework
- Lifelong companion

The program seeks depth, rigor, sincerity, and continuous growth.

## Mission

The mission of the program is to:

- Deepen understanding of the Quran.
- Build a lifelong study habit.
- Connect verses with lived experience.
- Explore multiple scholarly perspectives.
- Integrate reflection and action.
- Cultivate wisdom and character.

The mission is to deepen:

- Understanding
- Reflection
- Practice
- Character
- Wisdom

The ultimate goal is transformation rather than information accumulation.

## Study Methodology

The Quran should be studied through multiple complementary lenses:

- Linguistic
- Historical
- Theological
- Philosophical
- Ethical
- Civilizational
- Comparative
- Scientific Reflection
- Future Studies
- Personal Development

No single lens should be considered sufficient on its own.

## Core Study Tracks

The program consists of multiple complementary study tracks.

## Track 1: Traditional Quranic Study

Focus areas:

- Tafsir
- Asbab al-Nuzul
- Quranic Sciences
- Hadith Context
- Classical Scholarship
- Contemporary Scholarship

Major resources and scholars include:

- Al-Mizan
- Allamah Tabataba'i
- Al-Tabari
- Al-Qurtubi
- Ibn Kathir
- Fakhr al-Din al-Razi
- Contemporary academic scholarship

## Track 2: Quranic Arabic

Focus areas:

- Vocabulary
- Root words
- Morphology
- Syntax
- Rhetoric
- Balagha


- Christian theology

## Track 4: Philosophical Study

Guiding questions:

- What is knowledge?
- What is truth?
- What is justice?
- What is wisdom?
- What is human nature?
- What is freedom?
- What is consciousness?

Connections should be made to:

- Plato
- Aristotle
- Al-Farabi
- Ibn Sina
- Mulla Sadra
- Contemporary philosophy

## Track 5: Scientific Reflection

Focus areas:

- Observation
- Nature
- Natural patterns
- Cosmology
- Natural order
- Human inquiry

Guiding principle:

Avoid pseudoscience and forced scientific interpretations.

The objective is reflection rather than miracle hunting.

Avoid:

- Pseudoscience
- Forced scientific interpretations
- Miracle hunting

## Track 6: Civilizational Study

Focus areas:

- Education
- Society
- Community
- Governance
- Economics
- Law
- Ethics
- Knowledge systems

Examine how Quranic principles influenced civilization-building.

## Track 7: Future Studies

Questions include:

- Artificial intelligence
- Technological change
- Emerging technologies
- Environmental stewardship
- Future civilizations
- Human flourishing

Repository structure:

```text
quran/future-studies/
```

- What weakness should I reduce?

Reflection should lead to action.
